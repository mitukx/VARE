# Engineering depth: contracts, failures and evidence

This guide maps design choices to **code, regression tests and retained observations**. It distinguishes reproduced CPU failure modes from unmeasured model, throughput or deployment claims. The [architecture](architecture.md) gives the component map; the [script index](../scripts/README.md) provides executable entry points.

| Failure boundary | Design choice / tradeoff | Code and checks | Retained evidence / remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concurrent rollouts and round interference | Bound rollout concurrency; serialize rounds sharing mutable hooks. Extra serialization limits throughput but prevents overlapping candidate lifetimes. | [Engine](../src/vare/engine.py), [coordinator](../src/vare/runtime.py), [ownership tests](../tests/test_runtime_round_ownership.py) | [Ten-case baseline/fix replay](runtime-round-ownership-v1.md): eight targeted pinned-main failures, all ten corrected passes including controls. Cooperative single-host ownership only. |
| Stale or malformed training signals | Version-fence policy and verifier data, keep replay groups intact, recheck freshness under the trainer's model lock. Rejection consumes opportunities rather than silently optimizing a stale signal. | [Lag](../src/vare/lag.py), [replay](../src/vare/replay.py), [RVL adapter](../src/vare/integrations/rvl_grpo.py), [replay tests](../tests/test_replay_group_freshness.py) | [Direct-trainer stale-source replay](rvl-direct-trainer-freshness-v1-report.md); [singleton group guard](rvl-grpo-singleton-group-v1-report.md). Signal-path correctness, not learning gains. |
| Failure or cancellation during evaluation | Drain owned sibling tasks **before** candidate discard; preserve the first exception. A noncooperative hook can prevent timely progress, so there is no hard cleanup deadline. | [Engine](../src/vare/engine.py), [lifecycle tests](../tests/test_engine_evaluation_lifecycle.py) | [13-case comparison](engine-evaluation-lifecycle-v1-report.md), including an independently surfaced and corrected cancellation counterexample. |
| Ambiguous candidate publication | Require active-policy acknowledgment and, when configured, checkpoint digest/journal agreement. Refuse further work on uncertain publication rather than assuming rollback. | [Gate](../src/vare/promotion.py), [journal](../src/vare/promotion_journal.py), [publication tests](../tests/test_promotion_publication_recovery.py) | [Publication recovery](promotion-publication-recovery-v1.md) and [owned cancellation](promotion-cancellation-ownership-v1.md). External optimizer/tensor state is not atomically committed with SQLite. |
| Host death or duplicate evaluator completion | Use SQLite transactional claims, expiring leases and fencing tokens; evaluate **at least once**, but prevent superseded attempts from publishing effective results. | [Durable store](../src/vare/durable.py), [tests](../tests/test_durable.py) | [Recovery contract](recovery.md). Expired work may execute again; detached side effects are not rolled back. |
| Malformed graders or changing source | Bound execution time/output, verify protected inputs before/after, reject invalid output and persist raw evidence. Bounds and repeated validation impose cost and cannot exclude hostile races. | [Bounded runner](../src/vare/runner.py), [tests](../tests/test_runner.py) | [Execution contract](execution.md) and [recovery report](recovery-report.md). Not a security sandbox. |
| Oversized or nonregular checkpoint inputs | Open without blocking on FIFO and impose registered/caller read ceilings. Rejecting inputs has safety benefits but no demonstrated training throughput gain. | [Checkpoint store](../src/vare/checkpoint_artifacts.py), [bounded-read tests](../tests/test_checkpoint_read_bounds.py) | [IO report](checkpoint-read-bounds-v1-report.md): seven baseline failures; 15 corrected checks; five 32 MiB resource traces. |
| Reward hacking via untrusted action paths | Keep trajectory auditing opt-in and require independently bound evaluation receipts for promotion constraints. Trust root must be outside the candidate's privileges. | [Recorder/audit](../src/vare/trajectory_safety.py), [constraint](../src/vare/trajectory_promotion.py), [tests](../tests/test_trajectory_safety.py) | [Trajectory-safety scope](trajectory-aware-safety-v1.md). Does not intercept external I/O or establish performance against deployed malicious agents. |

## Reproducibility and performance interpretation

Retained **protocol locks** in [`protocols/`](../protocols/), raw [`results/`](../results/), and source-specific reports allow a reader to identify a pinned baseline, the tested fix, inputs, controls, and result artifacts. A test count in an old report describes the source revision tested **then**, not necessarily today's complete suite. CI definitions are in [`.github/workflows/`](../.github/workflows/); a passing workflow is not independent research replication.

The most concrete resource measurement in the top-level evidence is the [bounded checkpoint read](checkpoint-read-bounds-v1-report.md). No comparative end-to-end RL throughput, multi-host scalability, accelerator utilization, or production failure-rate result is established here. A crash fence, validation check, and synchronous SQLite journal each carry latency or availability tradeoffs; do not infer an efficiency win without a matched benchmark.

## Research-result boundary

The [model study index](experiments.md) records failed and inconclusive comparisons rather than turning fixture-correctness tests into policy quality claims. [OpenBookQA](openbookqa-qwen25-grpo-sft-confirmation-v1-report.md) retired its specific model/task/reward pairing; the [format-versus-correctness record audit](format-comparison-record-v1-report.md) retains a confidence interval including zero and incomplete original execution provenance. The [research admission review](research-candidate-qualification-2026-10-15.md) did not qualify a fresh model/task pair. The next scientific result would require preregistered untouched confirmation tasks, matched no-update/SFT/RL controls, multiple seeds and independent scoring—not additional proxy-reward curves.

## Suggested review path

Run the [CPU quick start](../README.md#quick-start), inspect the [engine](../src/vare/engine.py) and [hook contract](../src/vare/interfaces.py), then follow **one** pinned baseline/fix report into its regression test and raw artifacts. For external-runtime recovery, follow the [operator procedure](runtime-round-ownership-v1.md#operator-recovery) rather than assuming a passing test restores an actual checkpoint.
